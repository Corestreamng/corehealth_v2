## Overview

![Clinical AI Scribe Studio Live Interface](/images/workbenches/clinical-ai-scribe-studio.jpg)

Release `v2.6.0.68` introduces the **Clinical AI Scribe Studio ("Full AI Mode")**, an ambient clinical documentation and order extraction cockpit embedded directly across Doctor New Encounter, Nursing Workbench, and Maternity Workbench.

## Key Enhancements

1. **Ambient Voice Dictation & 60 FPS Waveform**:
   - Web Audio API `AnalyserNode` driving a real-time 60 FPS audio visualizer styled with `--hospital-primary` (`var(--hospital-primary, #0066cc)`).
   - Word streaming with interim transcript rendering and blinking caret (`|`).
2. **Multi-Dialect & Language Matrix**:
   - Direct support for all system languages in `resources/lang/` (`en`, `fr`, `es`, `ar`, `pt`, `nl`, `zh`) alongside Nigerian indigenous and regional dialects (`en-NG`, `pcm-NG`, `ha-NG`, `yo-NG`, `ig-NG`).
3. **Dynamic Clinical Taxonomy Switcher & Lossless Translation**:
   - **Doctors**: Traditional Nigerian Clinical Clerking (`[PC]`, `[HPC]`, `[ROS]`, `[PMH]`, `[Exam]`, `[Impression]`, `[Plan]`) as the default standard, with switcher pills for `[SOAP]` and `[POMR]`.
   - **Nurses**: Focus `[DAR]` (Data, Action, Response) as the default standard, with switcher pills for `[SBAR]` and `[SOAPIE]`.
   - **Maternity**: Journey-aware clinical headings (`Antenatal Care`, `Labor & Delivery`, `Postnatal Care`, `Neonatal Assessment`).
   - Lossless cross-format conversion and additive incremental dictation with zero information loss.
4. **Catalog Grounding & Schema-Constrained Tool Calling**:
   - `OrderCatalogRegistryService` provides token-optimized categories and fuzzy matching against CoreHealth's `services` and `products` catalogs.
   - Extracts laboratory, imaging, pharmacy prescriptions, procedures, specialist referrals, and follow-up appointments.
   - Safety allergy cross-checking flags potential patient adverse reactions.
5. **Interactive Detailed Orders & Prescriptions Deck**:
   - Table manager allowing inline adjustment of dosages, frequencies, quantities, and instructions before sign-off.
   - Closed-loop dispatch through CoreHealth's standard manual workbench order APIs on `Accept & sign`.
6. **Regulatory Compliance & Draft Persistence**:
   - Enforces `Draft · clinician review required` governance.
   - Auto-saves state to `sessionStorage` and `localStorage` on a 500ms debounced timer, surviving reloads and tab closures.
   - Archives immutable audit trail in `ai_scribe_audits`.
