## Overview

Release `v2.6.0.66` resolves billing cockpit account panel stacking conflicts and introduces dual prefix selection for antenatal and postnatal care.

## Key Enhancements
1. **Billing Account Cockpit Scaling**:
   - Repositioned `#account-transaction-panel` above `#account-stats-grid` with smooth accordion action.
   - Eliminates form overlapping on 1366×768 low-resolution monitors.
2. **Dual Maternity Enrollment Prefixes**:
   - Integrated `ANC-` (Antenatal) and `PANC-` (Postnatal) prefix switcher pills in `#patientFormModal`.
   - Real-time sequential numbering previews.
