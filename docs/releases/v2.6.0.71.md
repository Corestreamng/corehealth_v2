# Release v2.6.0.71

**Date**: 2026-11-12  
**Git Tag**: `v2.6.0.71`  
**Domain**: Inventory Double-Entry Accounting, Express Stock Receipt / Direct Purchase (Practice A), Audited Physical Count Variance GL Integration, Batch Governance (Locked Quantity Invariant), "Recorded in Error" Voiding Architecture, Manual Batch Returns & Unbilled Departmental Consumption  

---

## Overview

Release `v2.6.0.71` resolves the fundamental architectural disconnect between physical warehouse tally card operations and financial general ledger accounting in CoreHealth v2. It implements **"Practice A: Express Stock Receipt / Direct Purchase"**, enabling hospital storekeepers to preserve their streamlined, 1-step direct intake workflow while the system automatically handles strict double-entry General Ledger accounting in the background.

Additionally, this release enforces the **Locked Quantity Invariant** on batch metadata edits, provides an audited **"Recorded in Error" Voiding Architecture** (preventing non-existent data-entry mistakes from falsely distorting shrinkage and operating loss metrics), delivers **Manual Batch Supplier Returns** with multi-mode financial settlement, and integrates **Unbilled Ward Consumption** (`StockUtilization`) directly into the hospital cost of medical supplies ledger.

---

## Key Enhancements

### 1. Express Stock Receipt / Direct Purchase ("Practice A")
- **Preserved Storekeeper Velocity**: Preserves the 1-step direct receipt flow on the Tally Card workbench (`/inventory/store-workbench/tally-card`), avoiding multi-step procurement bureaucracy for routine stock receipts.
- **Mandatory Registered Supplier Selection**: Requires selection of an authenticated vendor (`supplier_id`) from the hospital supplier catalog, eliminating orphaned intake debts.
- **Acquisition Type Accounting Matrix (`StockBatchAccountingObserver`)**:
  - **On Credit (Supplier Debt - Default)**: Posts **`DR 1310/1300 Store Inventory Asset`** and **`CR 2100 Accounts Payable`** tagged directly to the vendor's dedicated subsidiary ledger sub-account.
  - **Petty Cash / Till**: Posts **`DR 1310/1300 Store Inventory Asset`** and **`CR 1030 Petty Cash / Cash in Hand`**, reflecting immediate cash outflow from store custody.
  - **Opening Stock (Equity)**: Posts **`DR 1310/1300 Store Inventory Asset`** and **`CR 3100 Retained Earnings (Opening Equity)`**, cleanly capitalizing baseline warehouse holdings without false liabilities.
  - **Free Donation / Program Stock**: Enforces `is_donation = 1` and locks cost to `₦0.00`, logging physical inventory increments without generating unpayable liabilities.

### 2. Batch Metadata Governance & Locked Quantity Invariant
- **Editable Metadata (`Modal 10`)**: Storekeepers can update lot number, lot label/name, supplier assignment, expiry date, and unit cost price on active batches.
- **Locked Quantity Invariant**: The `current_qty` and `initial_qty` fields are strictly locked against direct in-place mutation. Any physical count change must pass through the audited physical count adjustment flow.

### 3. Audited Physical Count Adjustments & Double-Entry Variance (`StockAdjustmentAccountingObserver`)
- **Surplus (+)**: Positive variance found during bin audits logs **`DR 1310 Store Inventory Asset`** and **`CR 4230 Stock Variance Surplus Gain (Other Income)`**.
- **Shortage (-)**: Missing or unlocated units log **`DR 5050 Inventory Shrinkage Loss (Operating Expense)`** and **`CR 1310 Store Inventory Asset`**.

### 4. "Recorded in Error" Voiding Architecture
- **Batches Recorded in Error (`Modal 11`)**:
  - Intended strictly for lots entered by mistake that never physically existed (e.g., duplicated invoice entry or wrong drug selected).
  - Requires mandatory explanation note, zeroes remaining lot quantity, and deactivates the batch.
  - Automatically invokes `AccountingService::reverseEntry` to swap debits and credits and mark the intake entry as `STATUS_REVERSED`.
  - **Zero Metric Distortion**: Prevents non-existent data-entry errors from being counted as physical warehouse shrinkage or operating loss.
- **Adjustments Recorded in Error (`Modal 13`)**:
  - Accessible directly on adjustment rows in the Tally Card transaction history via the <i class="mdi mdi-undo-variant"></i> **Void** button.
  - Restores the original lot balance and cleanly reverses the variance gain/loss journal entry.

### 5. Manual Batch Returns to Suppliers (`StockBatchReturnObserver`)
- Provides an audited return pipeline for direct intake batches, mirroring purchase order returns.
- **Settlement Modes**:
  - `ap_credit_note`: **`DR 2100 Accounts Payable`** (reduces vendor debt on subsidiary sub-account) / **`CR 1310 Inventory`**.
  - `cash_refund`: **`DR 1010 Cash in Hand`** / **`CR 1310 Inventory`**.
  - `receivable`: **`DR 1200 Accounts Receivable`** / **`CR 1310 Inventory`**.
  - `no_financial_impact`: Reverses physical stock on donated/grant stock with zero monetary credit.

### 6. Unbilled Departmental & Ward Consumption (`StockUtilizationObserver`)
- Captures internal hospital consumables (e.g., inpatient wound dressings, cleansing fluids, immunization consumables) consumed without direct patient billing.
- Automatically posts **`DR 5020 Cost of Medical Supplies Used (Departmental Expense)`** and **`CR 1310 Inventory - Medical Supplies`**.

### 7. Real-Time Double-Entry UI Preview Widgets
- Added live double-entry posting preview cards across all storekeeper modals:
  - Add Batch (`Modal 2`)
  - Adjust Stock (`Modal 6`)
  - Record Store Damage (`Modal 7`)
  - Return Batch to Supplier (`Modal 12`)
  - Void Batch Reversal Preview (`Modal 11`)
