## Overview

Release `v2.6.0.67` introduces a multi-token keyword search engine for nursing consumables and clinical kits, solving middle-of-string query matching and clinical abbreviation typos.

## Key Enhancements
1. **`ClinicalSearchHelper`**:
   - Tokenizes search queries on whitespace and non-alphanumeric punctuation.
   - Matches keywords regardless of ordering in item descriptions (e.g., `wound` immediately locates `Private ward - wound dressing`).
2. **Clinical Abbreviation & Typo Resilience**:
   - Maps clinical variations (`wond` $\rightarrow$ `wound`, `inj` $\rightarrow$ `injection`, `canula` $\rightarrow$ `cannula`, `tab` $\rightarrow$ `tablet`, `syr` $\rightarrow$ `syrup`, `paracet` $\rightarrow$ `paracetamol`).
3. **Nursing Consumable Service Discovery**:
   - `NursingWorkbenchController@searchProducts` returns products and billable ward services with `[Product]` and `[Service]` taxonomy badges and automatic tab switching in BillingKit.
4. **Service Combo & Kit Bundle Inspection**:
   - Searches kit bundle items (`bundleItems.service` / `bundleItems.product`), enabling staff to find kits by typing any contained drug or consumable.
